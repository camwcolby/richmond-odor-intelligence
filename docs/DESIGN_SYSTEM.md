# Design system

## Typography
Use Aptos throughout the application.

## Core Inframark / WaterMinds palette
- Navy: `#0D004C`
- Blue: `#008FD5`
- Green: `#5EB95E`
- Lime: `#AFE327`
- Charcoal: `#45484D`
- Muted Blue: `#75AABB`
- Background: `#FFFFFF`

## Risk semantics
Brand colors remain primary. Operational alert states use:
- Low: Inframark Green `#5EB95E`
- Moderate: restrained amber `#E6A83A`
- High: muted red `#C84E4E`

Amber and red are intentionally reserved for operational risk states rather than general decoration.

## Branding hierarchy
1. WaterMinds is the product brand.
2. "Richmond Odor Intelligence" is the application title.
3. City of Richmond appears as the municipal partner / client mark.
4. Prototype / simulation disclosure remains visible but visually secondary.
